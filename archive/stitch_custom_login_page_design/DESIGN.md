## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an artisan French confectionery heritage merged with high-converting, rapid local digital commerce. The aesthetic communicates affordable luxury: disciplined culinary precision, bespoke visual craftsmanship, and absolute culinary purity (100% pure vegetarian and eggless).

The visual narrative pairs classical European editorial restraint with modern interactive clarity. Natural linen and cream backgrounds eliminate cold digital starkness, grounding the interface in warmth and organic elegance. Deep midnight navy anchors structural containers, while warm golden honey accents and rich confectionery tones (Valrhona cacao, raspberry blush, pure pistachio) guide attention through menus and ordering flows. 

The design combines classical typography, soft layered card elevations, and tactile micro-elements (weight selector pills, dual-action commerce buttons, and floating docks) to build an indulgent yet effortlessly clear browsing experience.

## Colors

The palette balances classic French editorial authority with appetizing warmth:

- **Primary (`#1C2B46`)**: Midnight Navy. Used for core structural anchors, top announcement surfaces, brand typography, primary action buttons, and dominant iconography. Communicates sophistication and heritage.
- **Secondary (`#D99B65`)**: Pâtisserie Gold / Warm Butter. Highlights artisan focal points, star ratings, CTA micro-accents, special tier ribbons, and hover highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Pure Veg Emerald. Communicates strict dietary purity (100% vegetarian & eggless certification) and quick-action ordering buttons (`Order Now ⚡`).
- **Neutral (`#EFE9DE`)**: Artisan Cream Canvas. Serves as the overarching background canvas, avoiding clinical white while providing gentle contrast against pure white and cream cards.

Named accent colors provide nuance:
- `cream-surface` (`#FAF7F2`) and `linen-card` (`#FFFFFF`) establish layered card elevations.
- `border-delicate` (`#DFD6C8`) is applied for ultra-fine product card strokes and dividers.
- `berry-rose` (`#E06D75`) highlights delicate fruit-infused highlights, specialty tags, and badge accents.
- `veg-badge-bg` (`#ECFDF5`) and `veg-badge-text` (`#065F46`) guarantee high-contrast legibility for dietary marks.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses an editorial, salon-like warmth while maintaining strict tabular legibility for rapid commerce.

- **Headline Font (Playfair Display)**: Delivers French patisserie elegance with classical transitional serifs. Used for high-profile hero titles, section headlines, storytelling quotes, and card product names. Display headlines frequently embrace italicized emphasis on evocative ingredient modifiers (e.g., *Korean Bento Boxes*, *Dark Truffle Cake*).
- **Body & Label Font (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Provides high-clarity geometric sans-serif shapes with humanist warmth. Ideal for compact multi-column product specs, ingredient summaries, variant pill buttons (`500 Gm`, `1 Kg`), and pricing metrics.
- **Micro-Copy & Overlines**: Uppercase tracked overlines (0.04em to 0.06em letter-spacing) emphasize dietary certifications, preparation guarantees, and category titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive 12-column grid system resting within a maximum container width of `1280px`.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Product listings default to 1 or 2 columns with horizontal chip scrolling. Persistent 64px mobile dock fixed to the bottom edge.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Product cards organize into 2 or 3 columns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with `3.5rem` margins and `1.75rem` gutters. Product catalog presents as a balanced 4-column grid. Form builders (e.g., "Design Your Dream Cake") adopt a 7/5 split layout between interactive form controls and sticky summary estimation cards.

Vertical rhythmic pacing uses `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) for intra-section components and `space-xxl` (`4rem`) between distinct narrative sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered, warm-tinted surfaces combined with soft, low-intensity shadows rather than cold grays:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background canvas filled with warm artisan linen `#EFE9DE`.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Cards use pure white `#FFFFFF` or subtle linen-cream `#FAF7F2`, bordered by a gossamer `1px solid #DFD6C8` stroke. Shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(28, 43, 70, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover States)**: Cards hovering or interactive panels lift gently: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 43, 70, 0.09)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Sticky Docks, Drawers & Modals)**: Fixed utility banners, the mobile dock, and the checkout slide-out panel use: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(28, 43, 70, 0.16)`.
- **Accent Glows**: Urgent conversion cues (`Order Now ⚡`) utilize an ambient primary-tinted reflection on hover: `0 4px 12px 0 rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced, approachable curvature with Level 2 roundedness (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Default UI Elements & Buttons**: `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius, balancing structural polish with softness.
- **Cards & Category Containers (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius, framing rich confectionery photography with refined containment.
- **Highlight Panels & Builders (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (`24px`) corner radius, creating welcoming framed sections.
- **Pill Elements (`rounded-full`)**: Complete border radius (`9999px`) applied to weight selector chips (`500 Gm`, `1 Kg`), dietary verification badges (`🟢 100% Veg`), and floating action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Order Button (`Order Now ⚡`)**: High-urgency emerald fill (`#10B981`), white text, bold weight, rounded 8px. Hover shifts to `#059669` with a subtle elevation glow.
- **Secondary Add to Basket (`+ Basket`)**: Deep Navy fill (`#1C2B46`), white text, rounded 8px. Hover shifts to `#2A3F66`.
- **Editorial Outlined / Studio Link**: Transparent background, `1.5px solid #1C2B46`, navy text, smooth scale transition on hover.
- **Hero CTA Pair**: Secondary gold button (`#D99B65`) alongside an emerald instant-order button.

### Badges & Dietary Tags
- **100% Veg Badge**: Pill-shaped container (`rounded-full`), `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` label with a prominent `🟢` indicator. Pinned to the top-left of every product card.
- **Editorial Badges**: Uppercase micro-tags with soft gold or berry borders for flags like `🍫 BELGIAN COUTURE` or `✨ AESTHETIC FAVORITE`.

### Product Cards
- Contained within `16px` rounded corners and a delicate `1px solid #DFD6C8` border.
- 1:1 or 4:3 high-definition photography with subtle zoom on card hover.
- Card body features title in Playfair Display (`18px`), rating badge in gold (`4.9 ★`), 2-line tasting notes in muted sans-serif, horizontal weight selector pills, and a split dual-button action footer (`+ Basket` | `Order Now ⚡`).

### Weight & Option Selectors (Chips)
- Pill buttons (`rounded-full`) with a crisp `1px solid #1C2B46` boundary.
- **Active state**: Solid navy background (`#1C2B46`) with white text.
- **Inactive state**: Transparent background with navy text; subtle hover fill with `#FAF7F2`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DFD6C8`, corner radius `8px`.
- Floating focus state transitions border to `#1C2B46` with a faint `0 0 0 3px rgba(28, 43, 70, 0.08)` focus ring.
- Labels in bold 12px Plus Jakarta Sans, featuring trailing maroon asterisks (`*`) for required fields.

### Custom Cake Builder Module
- 2-column framed section (`#FFFFFF` on `#EFE9DE` canvas). Left side contains drop-downs and input fields; right side displays a circular preview thumbnail alongside live price recalculation (`₹1,665`) and express dispatch notices.

### Navigation & Docks
- **Utility Bar**: Fixed top strip in deep navy `#142035` featuring running notification text and immediate contact callout (`+91 99094 44882`).
- **Mobile Floating Dock**: Fixed bottom bar with blurred backdrop surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`), 4 touch tabs (`Home`, `Cakes`, `Custom`, `Order Now`), and high-contrast active icons.
